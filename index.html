About CooperAI

CooperAI is a fan-made chat assistant focused entirely on Fisch, the open-world fishing adventure game on Roblox. It answers questions using a built-in knowledge base covering the game's core mechanics — no account, download, or data collection required.

CooperAI is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Roblox Corporation, WoozyNate, or the Fisching development team. Fisch and all related game content are the property of their respective owners.
