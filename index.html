About CooperAI

CooperAI is a fan-made chat assistant focused entirely on Fisch, the open-world fishing adventure game on Roblox created by developer WoozyNate and the Fisching team. It answers questions using a built-in knowledge base — including a 624-entry fish database with rarity, price, and catch locations — with no account, download, or data collection required.

CooperAI is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Roblox Corporation, WoozyNate, or the Fisching development team. Fisch and all related game content, fish names, and data are the property of their respective owners.
